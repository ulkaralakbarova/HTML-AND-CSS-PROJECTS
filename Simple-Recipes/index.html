Deep Dish Skillet Pizza

Prep Time:

30–40 minutes

Level:

Medium

About This Recipe

Deep dish pizza has a thick crust that is tender and chewy on the inside and nicely crisped on the outside. This simple recipe is perfect for lunch, dinner, or sharing with family and friends.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Your favourite pizza toppings
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Lightly coat a skillet with olive oil.
  3. Press the pizza dough into the skillet.
  4. Add the tomato sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  5. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  6. Allow the pizza to cool slightly before serving.
